# Acceptance checklist

Inspect the actual generated or edited image when accessible. Report only checks actually performed.

- Selected-concept fidelity: compare the actual image against the pre-production brief in `concept-fidelity.md`; require every essential defining feature to match, with observed evidence. Do not substitute the concept-selection score for an image assessment. Mark inaccessible evidence unverified.
- One clear visual idea and hero; purposeful optical balance; readable at mobile scale.
- Product identity matches supplied reference as closely as generation permits: silhouette, proportions, branding, controls, stand and screen details; no invented or duplicate product.
- Exact visible copy, spelling, Turkish characters, punctuation, dates and numbers match the brief; no invented features, price or claims.
- Brand palette and photographic language match supplied/approved guidance; no fabricated exact font compliance.
- Perspective, scale, support, contact shadows and reflections are believable.
- Bottom reserve is empty, full-width and low-detail, default 12%: no text, product, logos, props, stage, strong shadows/reflections, border or placeholder.
- No short/long decorative lines, underlines, overlines, dividers, ticks, brackets or text frames. Legitimate punctuation and real product/architectural features are not decorative strokes.
- All required content stays above the footer and within safe regions with breathing room.
- Target aspect and crop are appropriate. Establish exact dimensions or encoding only from available reliable metadata, never from a prompt or filename.
- Contrast is visibly strong; do not claim numeric accessibility measurements without actual evidence.
- All generation and corrections stayed in ChatGPT Image. No code-rendered or externally assembled replacement.

If a check fails, edit with ChatGPT Image and recheck. If unavailable or unverified, say so only as relevant; do not invent a pass. Prompt-only review checks instructions, not final pixels. A requested PNG or 1080 × 1350 is not proof of either property in the returned output.
