# Visual Reference Mining — operating playbook

Research prevents generic "AI premium" visuals, but must use real verifiable projects. If search is inaccessible, say so; don't invent reference research.

## 1 — Style-first search brief

Before searching, establish audience, desired perception, channel/dimensions, product/source invariants, approved branding and mandatory copy. Generate 3–5 STYLE queries, rather than a generic product-topic search.

Examples:
- site:behance.net luxury tech editorial negative space art direction
- site:behance.net swiss minimal typography product poster
- site:pinterest.com asymmetric editorial advertising layout muted palette
- site:behance.net conceptual product photography campaign
- site:fontsinuse.com high contrast grotesk editorial advertising
- site:are.na sculptural still life brand composition

## 2 — Mine 15–30 attributable references where available

Look at Behance, Pinterest, Dribbble, Awwwards, Are.na, Designspiration, Fonts in Use and Brand New. Prefer original designer case studies. For Pins, follow provenance when possible. Don't count 15 screenshots of one project as 15 independent references.

Use bd:search_engine and bd:scrape_as_markdown/html only **if actually accessible**. Otherwise use available site-aware web search and browser navigation. If resources are inaccessible, do not claim to have read or scraped them. Do not bypass authentication or terms restrictions.

## 4 — Pattern extraction

Ask: which color, type and layout principles recur; WHY do they work; which are meaningful for the audience? Frequency does not equal suitability. Choose one coherent grammar and reject contradictory effects. Vast white/negative space is not automatically premium when visual focus is weak.

## 5 — Inspiration ethics

Reinterpret broad composition, scale, lighting and spacing without duplicating distinct original artwork. If synthesizing 2–3 sources, borrow only general spatial, color and typography principles, add a new concept and brand-specific execution. Never reuse unlicensed creator photos/illustrations/logos or create recognizable near-copies.

## 6 — Gate

Verify actual relevance, attribution and originality. Check brand/source-product constraints, typography-safe zone, single hero idea and absence of copied distinctive layouts. When research quality is low, state limitation and do not oversell pattern confidence.
