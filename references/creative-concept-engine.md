# Module 02 — Creative Concept Engine

**Activate for:** undistinguished briefs, product launch key visuals, creative scenes beyond the default pedestal or generic studio.

## Concept extraction

1. Note verified product truth, client-approved brand positioning, audience motivation and desired feeling. Never invent marketing claims.
2. Translate an actual attribute into an emotional/visual analogy (clarity → perceptual precision, speed → compressed movement, calm → still architecture).
3. Develop three genuinely different visual languages. Each needs a sentence-long thesis, hero footprint, metaphor, setting, core material, light signature and text-safe zone.
4. Use concept-evaluation.md with evidence, blockers and weighted scores. Reject ideas that require source product distortion or crowd out legible copy.
5. Execute ONE strong idea and remove decoration with no conceptual role.

## Moves

- Functional metaphor: show meaning without unsupported functional claims.
- Material juxtaposition: contrast technology with tactile physical materials.
- Architectural reveal: integrate product into a credible niche or visual aperture.
- Controlled scale: make subject unusually strong without deforming proportions.
- Color silence: restrained neutrals plus authentic brand accent.
- Context inversion: surprising setting that still communicates the product.

Avoid indiscriminate rings, floating particles, arbitrary neon, smoke, incorrect physical implications, and false product specifications. For price-led promotions, prioritize readability without creating sticker clutter.

## Reference ethics

Mine structure, spacing, lighting and typographic logic, not copyrighted photographs, logos, illustrations or near-identical layouts. Record real source URLs only after actually reviewing them.
