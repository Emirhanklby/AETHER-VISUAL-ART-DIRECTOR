# Module 02 — Creative Concept Engine

**Activate for:** generic or unoriginal briefs, launch key visuals, product campaigns, requests for a creative setting beyond an ordinary product-on-platform scene.

## Concept extraction (not decoration)

1. List verified product truth, user-supplied brand positioning, audience motivation, and desired feeling. Do not fabricate feature/price claims.
2. Translate a **real attribute** into an emotional or physical property (e.g., clarity → perceptual precision; speed → compressed motion; calm → architecture that feels still).
3. Generate three distinct directions in different visual languages. Each must have a one-sentence thesis, hero/product footprint, metaphor, environment, dominant material, light signature and copy-safe zone. Distinct means genuinely different compositional systems, not three palette variants.
4. Run [`concept-evaluation.md`](concept-evaluation.md) using evidence, risk flags and weighted ranking. Eliminate concepts that conflict with source product geometry or make the ad unreadable.
5. Keep one hero idea; remove supporting elements that add no meaning.

## Concept moves

- **Functional metaphor:** imply product benefit through environmental effect without inventing specifications.
- **Material juxtaposition:** tactile real materials challenge a clean technology product.
- **Architectural reveal:** product integrated into an intentional spatial aperture or niche.
- **Scale relationship:** a hero occupies an unexpected but coherent amount of space; avoid product distortion.
- **Color silence:** dominant neutral field plus one real brand accent.
- **Context inversion:** familiar product shown in an unfamiliar but still understandable place.

Avoid generic glowing rings, gravity-defying decorations, arbitrary smoke, and false implications that a real product can perform a depicted fantasy. If campaign intent is price-led, keep promotion readable but not sticker-cluttered.

## Originality boundary

Research references for design grammar (lighting, spacing, structure), not for reusing protected photography, artwork, logo or an identifiable near-duplicate layout. Document URL-level influences when sources were actually reviewed.
